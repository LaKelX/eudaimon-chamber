How renting works

Everything runs on GitHub: the booking desk is an issue form, the chamber is a GitHub Actions runner, and every session is stored in the repo.

1

Book

Open a booking issue. Choose how many years each brain trains, how many different worlds it trains across, and how many brains to train.

2

Approval

The keeper reviews it and adds the approved label. Only maintainers can label, so nobody can run the chamber without a yes.

3

Training

A runner opens the door. Each brain lives through its years of booms, crashes and regime shifts, learning which of eight theories fits which market.

4

The exam

Each trained brain and a newborn face 12 years of a world neither has seen. Results post to your issue, the brains are saved as JSON, and the session joins the board below.

What the chamber has shown so far

From the founding sessions on Nov 16, 2026, 8 brains each. Small samples; every new session adds evidence.

Learning is real, then it plateaus

Brains start near zero skill and reach about +35% within roughly two decades inside. After that, more years add little. Time alone is not wisdom.

The edge transfers, but it's small

In unseen worlds, trained brains beat newborns by about +0.4 to +2.6 points of skill. Positive, not dramatic: newborns learn fast too.

Variety beats duration

The 16-world "grand tour" had the most consistent exam wins over 12 years (7 of 8 brains). One long world mostly helped early, then the edge faded.

Why the ceiling

A brain's archetype library fills at 12 market types, so a veteran can over-fit the worlds it grew up in. Raising that cap is the next experiment.
